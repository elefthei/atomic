## Key gallery/copy patterns extracted
- Minimum 3 images recommended; successful launches use 4–7
- First image = OG/social preview; doubles as the hero card on the PH front page
- Gallery narrative arc: intro/hero → product workflow shots (1 idea per frame) → outcome/metric proof → CTA
- No body copy in images; text is overlaid as short headlines or callout labels
- Taglines: hard 60-char limit; all examples are declarative outcome phrases, 5–9 words
- "Benefit and hint" pattern: text states benefit, visual shows relevant UI segment (not full-screen screenshot)
- CTA slide always last — one defined next action
- Screenshots of actual developer environments (IDE panels, terminal, dashboard) dominate feature slides
- Zero examples of body copy or explanatory paragraphs in gallery images
- Novel products (Layercode, Warp agentic) still keep slides spare; education happens in first comment
